Chaibreak

Quick, satisfying single-player games for short breaks. A free Chrome extension with five games: Zest, Julienne, Slab, Pinpoint and Pocket.

How to play

Install the extension and click the Chaibreak icon in the toolbar, or press Alt+Shift+C. Pick a game and click or press Space to play. Pop it out into its own window to move and resize it.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no permissions, works offline. Read the full privacy policy.
