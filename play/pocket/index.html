    Clear the table.

    A quick pool game. Six shots to pot as many balls as you can. Aim with the pointer, hold to charge, release to shoot.

    How to play

    1. Move the pointer to aim, or use the left and right arrow keys.
    2. Hold click, touch or Space to charge the shot, then release.
    3. A shot with no pot costs one shot. Clear the table for a bonus.
