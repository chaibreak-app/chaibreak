Chaibreak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026. Applies to the Chaibreak Chrome extension, version 1.0.0 and later until replaced.

The short version

Chaibreak does not collect, share or sell your personal data. It does not connect to the internet, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it never reads or changes the websites you visit.

What stays on your device

To work as a game, Chaibreak saves a few small items in your browser's local storage on your own computer: your best score for each game, your daily play streak, whether sound is muted, your theme choice, and your preferred pop-out window shape and size. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer. You can erase it at any time with the "Clear saved data" button on the Chaibreak home screen, or by removing the extension.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, account details or identifiers.
  • No browsing history, website content, location, or device information.
  • No cookies and no third-party trackers or advertising code.
  • No payment information. Chaibreak has no purchases.

Permissions

Chaibreak requests no browser permissions. When you choose to pop it out, it opens its own page in a separate browser window using Chrome's built-in window feature, which needs no permission and shares no data.

Third parties

Chaibreak does not send data to any third party. All code, graphics and sounds are included in the extension package and run locally, including offline.

Children

Chaibreak is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Contacting us

If you email the support address below, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to reply and delete them on request. Questions, concerns or requests about this policy: chaibreak.app@gmail.com

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, for example by adding an optional tip link, this policy will be updated first, the change will be described in the Chrome Web Store listing, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
